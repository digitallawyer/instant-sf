Privacy

Nothing to track.

The short version: no accounts, no analytics, no ads, no cookies. Your rides stay on your device.

What we collect

Nothing. There is no sign-up, no login and no analytics or advertising script on this site or in the game. We don’t build profiles and we don’t sell or share data, because we don’t have any.

What stays in your browser

A few preferences can be saved in your browser’s local storage so they’re still there next time, such as your chosen language on this website. This data never leaves your device. You can clear it at any time by clearing this site’s data in your browser settings; everything still works without it.

What your browser downloads

  • The city. The map and elevation files are served from this site, like any other page asset.
  • Fonts. The Inter and Instrument Serif typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives your IP address and browser details as part of serving them. See Google’s privacy policy.
  • Links out. Links to OpenStreetMap and other sites take you to services with their own privacy policies.

Like any web server, the host may keep standard technical logs (such as IP address and requested page) for security and to keep the site running. We don’t use them to identify you.

Children

The game is suitable for all ages and asks nobody for personal information.

Changes

If this ever changes, for example if online races arrive, this page will be updated first and will say exactly what is stored and why.
