The game →

Real buildings, real height

More than forty thousand buildings from OpenStreetMap, each extruded to its measured height and roof shape.

Hills you can feel

The terrain comes from USGS 3DEP elevation. On the bike, a 17% grade on Hyde Street really does slow you down.

Bike, board, feet or flippers

Ride a bike, push a skateboard, walk, or flop along the Embarcadero as a sea lion from Pier 39.

Karl the Fog

Fog rolls in through the Gate and swallows the towers. Clear afternoon, foggy morning, it’s up to Karl.

Day and night

A full day cycle with the sun where it should be over the Bay, and thousands of lit windows after dark.

Hop on a cable car

Cable cars creep up the Powell and California lines at cable speed. Press E next to one to jump on the running board and ride it over the hill.

A city that’s awake

Traffic that stops at working signals, people on the sidewalks, sea lions on the Pier 39 docks, ferries and sailboats on the Bay, and street lamps that come on at dusk.

Races, missions and a tour

Race three rivals over the hills with fortune-cookie power-ups, beat the clock on five missions, or sit back for a 13-stop tour from a foggy dawn at the Golden Gate to night over the skyline.
